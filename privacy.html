This policy explains how Dream Studio Print handles personal information submitted through this website or through direct contact.

Information we collect

When you contact Dream Studio Print or submit a quote request, you may provide your name, business name, email address, phone number, city or province, project requirements and other information you choose to include.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, discuss design and production requirements, provide services, and maintain business records related to requested work.

Analytics and site preferences

This website may use basic, privacy-focused traffic analytics if you choose to allow analytics. Your analytics preference is stored in your browser so the site can remember your choice.

Website forms and email delivery

Quote requests submitted through this website are processed by website hosting and email-delivery services so the inquiry can be delivered to Dream Studio Print. Form information is transmitted over HTTPS and is not intentionally exposed in website source code.

Sharing

Dream Studio Print does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the website, deliver an inquiry, communicate with you, process a project, or deliver requested services.

Retention

Inquiry and project information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, quoting, production records, legal obligations, and business administration.
